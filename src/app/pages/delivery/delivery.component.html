How it works

  1. The customer picks delivery, chooses their area and enters their address. They see the fee before they order.
  2. You get the order on WhatsApp with the address, phone number, items and fee.
  3. In Bookings and orders, mark it Preparing, then Out for delivery. The customer sees each step on their order page.
  4. The customer gets a 4-digit code on that page. When the rider hands over the order, the customer gives the code and you enter it to mark the order Delivered.
